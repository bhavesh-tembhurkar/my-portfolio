About Me

I am an iOS and macOS developer driven by a desire to challenge the limitations of the Apple ecosystem. While Apple devices offer incredible hardware and security, they often come with strict restrictions that limit user freedom. My goal is to craft native applications that break through these barriers—giving users the power to do things they previously couldn't, without compromising on privacy or performance.

With deep knowledge of the Apple ecosystem, I strive to write code that scales seamlessly across iOS, macOS, watchOS, and visionOS. A prime example of this philosophy is my project Orga. Frustrated by the lack of secure, truly private file vaults and the rigid restrictions of mainstream browsers (like limited video downloading), I built Orga to provide uncompromised file encryption and a deeply customizable, on-device private browsing experience.

Having recently completed an internship at Cluematrix Technologies, where I built and deployed scalable native applications to the App Store, I am now actively seeking full-time opportunities to engineer innovative solutions that push the boundaries of what Apple devices can do.
