Orga. A secure, native vault built for macOS.

Simple, secure, and built specifically for your Mac. Keep your sensitive documents encrypted, hidden, and completely under your control.

Download for macOS

Quick installation.

Get up and running on your Mac in under a minute. No complicated setup, no hidden steps.

  1. Download zip archive

    Click the download button above to get the latest Orga.zip bundle.

  2. Extract Orga app

    Double-click the zip archive in Finder to extract the native executable Orga.app.

  3. Move to Applications

    Drag the extracted Orga.app file straight into your macOS Applications directory.

  4. Launch and secure

    Open the app via Launchpad, set up your vault master password, and start encrypting files.

Features designed for safety.

Orga blends system-level security controls with native Swift frameworks to provide robust, local-first file privacy without external dependencies.

One-click stealth lock.

Keep your sensitive files completely out of sight in less than a second. Orga lets you instantly obscure files, making them invisible in Finder searches, lists, and Terminal:

  • Instant Invisibility: Hide folders or documents immediately with a single click.
  • Safe from Prying Eyes: Perfect for locking everyday files when sharing your Mac with family or colleagues.
  • Dual Protection: Swap seamlessly between rapid stealth hiding and industrial-grade AES encryption.
Orga Vault Manager

Touch ID security.

Access your vaults securely without typing long, complex passwords every time you open a file:

  • Biometric Unlock: Touch the sensor on your Mac keyboard to open vaults instantly.
  • No Stored Passwords: Credentials are salted, hashed, and never saved in plain text.
  • Keychain Lock: Integration with macOS system Keychains secures encryption keys at the hardware level.
Vault Locked

Zero traces left behind.

Work confidently knowing Orga handles security cycles in the background, cleaning up after you:

  • Inactivity Watchdog: The app detects if you step away and automatically locks vaults after 5 minutes.
  • Self-Destruct Cache: Temporary sandboxed preview files are permanently deleted on lock.
  • Safe Launches: Fully wipes temporary directories every time you open or quit the app.
Settings

Fast shortcuts & native sorting.

Orga feels like a natural extension of your operating system. Navigate files with native speed and ease:

  • Keyboard Hotkeys: Lock, unlock, and manage files with fast keyboard shortcuts.
  • Smart Category Sorting: Organize and filter vault items instantly by file type (Images, Videos, Documents, Archives).
  • Designed for macOS: Sleek sidebar, clean window frames, and standard visual transitions built entirely in SwiftUI.
